Alert

Small Modal

This is a small modal. It's more compact and suitable for simple messages or small forms.

Large Modal

This is a large modal. It can accommodate more content, such as detailed information, larger forms, or multiple sections.

Additional Content

Large modals are useful when you need to display a lot of information or complex forms that wouldn't fit well in a smaller modal.

  • More space for content
  • Can include multiple sections
  • Better for complex forms
  • Good for displaying detailed information

Fullscreen Modal

This is a fullscreen modal. It takes up almost the entire screen and is useful for displaying large amounts of content or complex interfaces.

Use Cases

  • Image galleries
  • Complex forms with many fields
  • Document viewers
  • Admin panels or dashboards
  • Any content that needs maximum screen space
