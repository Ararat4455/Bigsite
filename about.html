Our Story

How It All Began

Founded in 2014, Bigsite started as a small team of passionate developers and designers with a shared vision: to create digital experiences that make a difference. What began as a humble startup has grown into a leading digital solutions provider, serving clients across the globe.

Over the years, we've had the privilege of working with amazing clients, from startups to Fortune 500 companies. Each project has been a learning experience, helping us refine our skills and expand our capabilities. Today, we're proud to have a diverse portfolio that showcases our expertise across various industries.
