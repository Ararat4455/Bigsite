Return Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2024

1. Our Return Policy

At Bigsite, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied with a product you purchased from us, you may return it for a refund or exchange within 30 days of the delivery date, subject to the conditions set forth below.

2. Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following criteria:

  • The item must be unused, in its original condition, and in the original packaging.
  • The item must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • You must have the receipt or proof of purchase.
  • The item must not be damaged, defective, or missing any parts or accessories.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Certain types of items cannot be returned, including:

  • Perishable goods such as food, flowers, or plants
  • Intimate or sanitary goods
  • Custom or personalized orders
  • Digital products or software downloads
  • Gift cards
  • Items marked as "final sale" or "non-returnable" at the time of purchase

4. Return Process

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact Customer Service: Reach out to our customer service team within 30 days of receiving your order to request a return authorization.
  2. Receive Return Authorization: Our customer service team will provide you with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return shipping instructions.
  3. Prepare Your Item: Place the item securely in its original packaging. Include all accessories, parts, and documentation that came with the item.
  4. Include Documentation: Include a copy of your receipt or proof of purchase, along with the RMA number, inside the package.
  5. Ship the Item: Ship the item to the address provided in the return shipping instructions. We recommend using a trackable shipping service or purchasing shipping insurance.

5. Return Shipping

You will be responsible for paying the return shipping costs. Return shipping costs will not be refunded. Depending on where you live, the time it may take for your exchanged product to reach you may vary.

If you receive a refund, the cost of return shipping will be deducted from your refund. If you are shipping an item over $75, you should consider using a trackable shipping service or purchasing shipping insurance. We don't guarantee that we will receive your returned item.

6. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If your return is approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within a certain number of days, depending on your card issuer's policies.

Please note that it may take up to 5-10 business days for your refund to appear on your account statement, depending on your bank or credit card company.

7. Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or model, please contact our customer service team to request an exchange. We will provide you with instructions on how to return your original item and receive the replacement.

Please note that exchange requests are subject to product availability. If the item you want is not in stock, we will issue a refund instead.

8. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact our customer service team within 7 days of receiving your order. We will arrange for a replacement or refund at no additional cost to you.

To report a damaged or defective item, please provide our customer service team with the following information:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the damage or defect
  • Photos of the damaged or defective item

9. Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received a refund yet, please check your bank account again. Then contact your credit card company, as it may take some time before your refund is officially posted. Next, contact your bank, as there is often some processing time before a refund is posted.

If you have done all of this and you still have not received your refund, please contact our customer service team, and we will investigate the issue.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Return Policy or need to initiate a return, please contact our customer service team:

  • By email: returns@bigsite.com
  • By phone: +1 (555) 123-4567
  • By visiting our contact page: Contact Us
