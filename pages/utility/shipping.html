Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2024

1. Domestic Shipping

We offer domestic shipping to all addresses within the United States. Our standard shipping rates and delivery times are as follows:

Shipping Method Delivery Time Cost
Standard Shipping 5-7 business days $5.99
Expedited Shipping 2-3 business days $12.99
Overnight Shipping 1 business day $24.99
Free Shipping 5-7 business days Free (orders over $50)

2. International Shipping

We offer international shipping to select countries. International shipping rates and delivery times vary depending on the destination country and the weight of your order. International shipping rates will be calculated at checkout.

Please note: International customers are responsible for all customs, duties, and taxes imposed by their country. These charges are not included in the purchase price or shipping cost and must be paid by the recipient upon delivery.

International delivery times may vary due to customs processing. We are not responsible for any delays caused by customs or other import procedures.

3. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.

During peak seasons or holidays, processing times may be extended. We will notify you via email if there are any significant delays in processing your order.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email that includes your tracking number and a link to track your package. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.

If you do not receive a tracking number within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact our customer service team for assistance.

5. Shipping Restrictions

Certain items may have shipping restrictions due to their size, weight, or nature. These restrictions may include:

  • Items that are hazardous, flammable, or otherwise restricted by shipping carriers
  • Items that require special handling or temperature control
  • Items that are too large or heavy to ship via standard carriers
  • Items that are restricted from being shipped to certain locations or countries

If an item in your order has shipping restrictions, we will notify you during the checkout process or via email after your order is placed.

6. Shipping Address

Please ensure that your shipping address is complete and accurate. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer. If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, please contact our customer service team as soon as possible.

Please note that we may not be able to change the shipping address once the order has been shipped.

7. Delivery Confirmation

We require a signature for all deliveries to ensure that your order is received by the intended recipient. If you are not available to sign for your package, the carrier may leave a notice of attempted delivery and return to attempt delivery again.

If no one is available to sign for the package after multiple delivery attempts, the package may be returned to us, and we will contact you to arrange for redelivery or a refund.

8. Multiple Shipping Addresses

Currently, we do not support shipping to multiple addresses in a single order. If you need to ship items to different addresses, please place separate orders for each shipping address.

9. Shipping to PO Boxes

We can ship to PO Box addresses using standard shipping methods. However, please note that expedited or overnight shipping may not be available for PO Box addresses.

10. Holiday Shipping

During the holiday season, we recommend placing your orders as early as possible to ensure timely delivery. Please note that shipping carriers may experience delays during peak holiday periods.

We will post our holiday shipping schedule and cutoff dates on our website to help you plan your holiday shopping.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Shipping Policy or need assistance with your order, please contact our customer service team:

  • By email: shipping@bigsite.com
  • By phone: +1 (555) 123-4567
  • By visiting our contact page: Contact Us
